# Xiaohu Ma

**Machine Learning · Sensor Fusion · Measurement & Control Engineering · Embedded Systems**

I am a doctoral researcher in Measurement and Control Engineering at the **University of Bayreuth, Germany**, with a background in mechatronics and industrial R&D.

My work connects **data-driven modeling and machine learning** with real physical systems: from automated sensor measurements and signal processing to simulation, model development, and experimental validation. I also enjoy building robotics and computer-vision projects outside my research work.

## Featured Projects

### [Automated Multi-Channel Impedance Spectroscopy](https://github.com/timeflowsinyou/Automated-MultiChannel-Impedance-Spectroscopy)
*Python · Analog Discovery 2 · Arduino Mega · Multiplexer Control*

An automated measurement platform for multi-frequency complex impedance acquisition across a 14-node network. Python coordinates the instrument and Arduino-controlled multiplexers, with reproducible data processing and visualization.

### [Impedance Sensor Network — Simulation & Sim-to-Real](https://github.com/timeflowsinyou/IEEE-TIM-Simulation-Only)
*Python · PyTorch · CNN · Physical Simulation*

Research exploration of simulated impedance-sensor networks, reduced measurement inputs, and the challenges of transferring trained models to real measurements. The repository documents both results and limitations.


### [ESP32 Self-Balancing Robot](https://github.com/timeflowsinyou/ESP32-Self-Balancing-Robot)
*Embedded C++ · ESP32 · MPU6050 · Sensor Fusion · PID*

A two-wheeled balancing robot combining IMU attitude estimation, complementary filtering, PID feedback, and motor control. The repository includes a physical robot demo and firmware for web-based PID configuration; the newer web features are not yet hardware-validated.

### [Touchless Robotic Piano Using Computer Vision](https://github.com/timeflowsinyou/Touchless-Robotic-Piano-Using-Computer-Vision)
*Python · OpenCV · MediaPipe · Arduino · Servo Motors*

A camera-based hand-tracking interface that maps finger movements to five servo motors, mechanically actuating piano keys through a USB-connected Arduino.

### [MediaPipe Moving Bubbles](https://github.com/timeflowsinyou/Mediapipe_movingbubble)
*Python · MediaPipe · OpenCV · Pygame*

An interactive hand-gesture-controlled animation with screen capture and dual-monitor support.
